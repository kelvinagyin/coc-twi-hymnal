Hymn 5

ODO KRONKRON
LOVE DIVINE

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Odo kronkron ekven 'do biara
Soro anigye aba fam ha
Fa yen mu ye wo tenabea
Ma wo ahummobo nni mu
Yesu wo ye awerekyekye
Wo ye do a enni ano
Fa wo nkwagye no besra yen
Bewura koma biara mu.

Koma biara a abre no
Home wo do honhom gu mu
Ma yen nya adedie wo mu
Ne ahomegye a woahye ho bo n
Yi bone ho do fi yen mu
Ne yen afodi adesoa no
Wie adwuma a woahye ase no
Fa yen ko daa nkwa no mu.

Tumfoo Agya bra begye yen
Ma yen nsa nka wo nkwa no
San bra na betena yen mu
Ngya wo ntomadan yi bio
Wo na yebehyira wo daa daa
Na yeasom wo se serspp dom no
Ye de mpaebo ne ayeyi a
Entwa da atena wo do mu.

Afei wie woabode foforo
Te yen ho fi nkekae nyinaa ho
Ma ye nhu nkwagye kese a
Wo anya ama yen no
Sesa yen ko wo anuonyam mu
Ma soro nye ye tenabea
Ma yento yeade ngu w'anim
Wo do ne ayeyi mu.



----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Love divine, all love excelling,
Joy of heav'n, to earth come down
Fix in us Thy humble dwelling
All Thy faithful mercies crown
Jesus, Thou art all compassion,
Pure, unbounded love Thou art;
Visit us with Thy salvation,
Enter ev'ry trembling heart.

Breath, o breathe Thy loving Spirit
Into ev'ry troubled breast;
May we all in Thee inherit
May we find the promised rest;
Take away the love of sinning,
Take our load of guilt away;
End the work of Thy beginning,
Bring us to eternal day.

Come, almighty to deliver,
May we all Thy life receive;
Suddenly return, and never,
Never more Thy temples leave;
Thee we would be always blessing,
Serve Thee as Thy hosts above
Pray, and praise Thee, without ceasing,
Glory in Thy perfect love.

Finish then Thy new creation,
Pure, unspotted, may we be;
May we see our whole salvation
Perfectly secured by Thee;
Changed form glory into glory,
Till in heav'n we take our place,
Till we cast our crowns before Thee,
Lost in wonder, love and praise
