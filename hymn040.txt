Hymn 40

GYE ME BRA
TAKE MY LIFE, AND LET IT BE

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Gye me bra me wura na ma
Memfa nhyɛ wo nsa koraa
Gye me merɛ ne me nnafua
Na memfa nyi wo aye daa.

Gye me nsa na ma menyɛ
Deɛ wo dɔ nko bɛkyerɛ, Gye ma nan na me ho
ɛnyɛ ha, fɛɛfɛ ma wo.

Gye me nne na ma mento
Nnwom ma me hene daa
Gye m'anofafa na ma
Menka wo nsɛmpa ara.

Gye me dwetɛ ne sika
Mennfa emu bi nkame wo
Gye m'adwen, fa m'ahoɔden
Yɛ deɛ wo ara hwɛ a ɛtene.

Gye me pɛ fa yɛ wo de
ɛrennyɛ me dea ara bio
Gye me koma, wo dea ampa
Mede bɛyɛ w'ahendwa.

Gye me dɔ m'Awurade
Mehwie gu wo nan ase
Gye me ne m'ade nyinaa
Mɛyɛ wo nko dea daa.


CHORUS:
Awurade me de me brɛ ma wo
ɛnyɛ wonko wo dea daa
Awurade me de mebra ma wo
ɛnyɛ wo nko dea daa.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Take my life and let it be consecrated, Lord, to Thee
Take my hands and let them move
at the impulse of Thy love.

Take my feet and let them be swift and beautiful for Thee;
Take my voice and let me sing
Always, only, for my King

Take my silver and my gold,
Not a mite would I withhold
Take my moments and my days,
Let them flow in ceaseless praise.
