Hymn 36

ME NIM DEƐ MAGYE NO ADI
I KNOW WHOM I HAVE BELIEVED

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Mennim sɛnti a Nyame ayi n'adom
Nwanwa akyerɛ me
Ana sɛ nti a me a memfata yi ɔde
Ne dɔ agye me.

Mennim sɛdeɛ ɔde ɔgye ɔgyidie
Yi hyɛɛ me mu
Ne sɛdeɛ n'asɛm no ho gyidie de
Asomdwee ba makomam.

mennim papa anaa bɔne a ɔde
Ato hɔ retwɛn me
Anaa ɔbrɛ akwan anaa nnepa
Ansa na m'ahu n'anim.

Sɛ me wura no bɛba anadwo anaa
Sɛ awia ketee a me deɛ mennim
Anaa sɛ me ne no bɛnante bun mu
Anaa sɛ mɛhyia no ewie mu.


CHORUS:
Nanso me nim deɛ magye n'madi
Na megyedi sɛ ɔbɛtumi de
Deɛ mede ahyɛ ne nsa no
Asie akɔdu da no.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

I know not why God's wondrous grace
To me He hath made known,
Nor why unworthy Christ in love
Redeemed me for His own.

I know just how this saving faith
To me He did impart,
And how believing in His word
Wrought peace within my heart.

I know not what of good or ill
May be reserved for me
Of weary ways or golden days
Before His face I see.

I know not when my Lord may come
At night or noon day fair,
Nor will I walk the vale with Him,
But meet Him in the air.
