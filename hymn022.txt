Hymn 22

YƐ BƐ SƐ NO


----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Se Agyenkwa no ba bɛfa ne nkorɔfoɔ a
Yɛ bɛ sɛ no, yɛ bɛ sɛ no
Se da ɛhyerɛn no pue na awufo nyane a
Yɛbɛ sɛ no, sɛ ɔba a.

Ma asɛm no ngyegye wɔ baabiara
Yɛ bɛ sɛ no, yɛ bɛ sɛ no
Ma ɛnkɔ akyiri asɛmpa no mu
Yɛbɛ sɛ no sɛ ɔba a.

Ɔno na yɛdɔ, ɔno na yɛahu
Yɛ bɛ sɛ no, yɛ bɛ sɛ no
ɔma yɛ anigye yɛ merɛyɛ mu
Yɛbɛ sɛ no sɛ ɔba a.

Sɛ ɔtwɛn mpo a, yɛn nnwom bɛyɛ sɛ
Yɛ bɛ sɛ no, yɛ bɛ sɛ no
ɔkwan mu yɛhu a, yɛbɛ sɔ ne mu
Yɛ bɛ sɛ no, sɛ ɔba a.


CHORUS:
Yɛbɛ sɛ no, to anigye dwom
Yɛbɛ sɛ no sɛ ɔba bio a
Anigyesɛm sɛ yɛbɛhu n'anim
Na yɛase no sɛ ɔba a.
