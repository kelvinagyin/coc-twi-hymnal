Hymn 9

WO ANIM HO
FARTHER ALONG

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Sohwe ne haw taa gya ye'adwenden se
Aden na ewo se eba saa mmere nyinaa
Bere a ebinom atwa yen ho ahyia
Wo ye bone nso haw nni won so.

Se owu ba befa ye'adofo ko
Egya awereho ne suro ne ye'afie mu
Na ema ye dwene won a asi won yie ho
So wote bone mu mmere nyinaa.

Yen wura no si di nokware ko wuo mu
Ye adwuma twen nna kakra yi mu
Na yerennu okwan mu suro no.
se

Se yehu Yesu se oba n'anuonyam
Se ofi ne soro fie no mu ba a
Yebeyhia no wo fie hyenhyen no mu
enkye bi yebete ne nyinaa ase.


CHORUS:
Anim ho ye behu bribiara
Anim ho yebete ne nyinaa ase
Ma w'anii nnye me nua, ka wo ho hye
Enkye bi yebete ne nyinaa ase.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Tempted and tried we're oft made to wonder
Why it should be thus all the day long,
While there are others living about us,
Never molested tho in the wrong,

When death has come and taken our loved ones,
It leaves our home so lonely and drear;
Then do we wonder why others prosper,
Living so wicked year after year.

Faithful till deathe said our loving Master,
A few more days to labor and wait;
Toils of the road will then seem as nothing,
As we sweep thru the beautiful gate.

When we see Jesus coming in glory,
When He comes from His home in the sky;
Then we shall meet Him in that bright mansion,
We'll understand it all by and by.
