Hymn 51

ANIGYE DA
O HAPPY DAY

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Anigye da, ɛne da no a
Mede me ho maa m'Agyenkwa
ɛsɛ sɛ me koma di de
Na ɔka ne de kyere aman.

Apam kɛseɛ no wawie pam
Me yɛ ne de, ɔyɛ me de
ɔtwetwee me, medii n'akyi
Ne Nyame nne no yɛ me de.

Afei me koma a wakyini akyɛ
Bɛhome wɔ homebea ha
Na mfi wo Awurade nkyɛn da
Wo fa no a woafa nhyira nyinaa.


CHORUS:
Nigye da, 'nigye da
Da a Yesu horoo me ho bɔne
ɛkyerɛɛ me sɛ menwɛn mmɔmpae
Na meni de bere nyinaa
Nigye da, 'nigye da
Da a Yesu horoo me ho bɔne.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

O happy day that fixed my choice,
On Thee my Savior and my God
Well may this glowing heart rejoice,
And tell its raptures all abroad.

Tis done, this great transaction's done,
I am the Lord's and He is mine
He drew me and I followed on
Charmed to confess the voice divine.

Now rest, my long divided heart
Fixed on this blissful center, rest;
Nor ever from my Lord depart,
With Him of every good possessed.
