Hymn 59

ƐYƐ ME DƐ SƐ YƐN AGYA WƆ SORO
JESUS LOVES EVEN ME

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

ɛyɛ me dɛ sɛ yɛn agya wɔ soro
Ka ne dɔ ho asɛm nhoma a ɔde mae m
Anwanwasɛm pii na mehu wɔ mu
Deɛ ɛkyɛn ne nyinaa ne sɛ Yesu dɔ me.

Yesu dɔ me na me nso medɔ no
Dɔ de no fii soro begyee me nkwa
Yiw ɛyɛ dɔ na ɔde wui dua no so
O minim pefee sɛ Yesu dɔ me.

Sɛ wɔbisabisa me a meka no sɛn?
Nuonyam nka Yesu, minim yiye sɛ
Nyame Honhom Kronkron ne me adom
Redi adanse sɛ Yesu dɔ me.

ɛdwom fua na ɛwɔ hɔ a mɛto
Bere a mehu ɔhene wɔ n'ahoɔfɛ m'
ɛyi beyɛ me dwom a mɛto daa
O anwanwansɛm, Yesu dɔ me.

Anidasoɔ yi mu, m'ahotɔ mu
Metwere Yesu a mewɔ nhyira
Satan ani bewu na ɔbɛdwane
Sɛ meka kyere no sɛ Yesu dɔ me.


CHORUS:
ɛyɛ me dɛ sɛ Yesu dɔ me
Yesu dɔ me, Yesu dɔ me
ɛyɛ me dɛ sɛ Yesu dɔ me
Yesu dɔ me sɛɛ yi.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

I am so glad that our Father in heaven
Tells of His love in the Book He has given
Wonderful things in the Bible I see,
This is the dearest that Jesus loves me.

Tho I forget Him and wander away,
Still He doth love me wherever I stray;
Back to His dear loving arms would I flee,
When I remember that Jesus loves me.

O there is only one song I can sing,
When in His beauty I see the great King
This shall my song in eternity be,
O what a wonder that Jesus loves me.
