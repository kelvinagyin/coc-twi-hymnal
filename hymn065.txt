Hymn 65

YEHOWA TUMFOƆ GYAA ME
GUIDE ME, O THOU GREAT JEHOVAH

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

O Yehowa Tumfoɔ gyaa me
Kwantuni wɔ sare so
Meyɛ mmerɛ, na wo ho ye den
Kura me wo tumi nsa mu
Soro Aduan
Yɛn me nnɛ ne da nyana.

Bue nsuaniwa hyɛnhyɛn no
Deɛ yaresa nsuo pem no
Ma ogya ne munumkum dum
Nya me makwantu nyinaa m'
Gyefoɔ Tumfoɔ
Yɛ me mmoa ne me kyɛm daa.

Sɛ medu Jordan konkɔ so a
Ma me suro nyinaa nsa
Wuo kumfoɔ asaman sɛefoɔ
Fa me kɔdu Kana sann
Ayeyie nnwom
Na mede bɛma wo daa.



----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Guide me, O thou great Jehovah,
Pilgrim through this barren land
I am weak but Thou art mighty,
Hold me with Thy pow'r hand;
Bread of heaven,
Feed me till I want no more

Open now the crystal fountain,
Whence the healing waters flow,
Let the fiery, cloudy pillar,
Lead me all my journey through
Strong Deliverer,
Be Thou still my strength and shield

When I tread the verge of Jordan
Bid my anxious fears subside
Bear me through the swelling current,
Land me safe on Canaan's side,
Songs of praises
I will ever give to Thee
