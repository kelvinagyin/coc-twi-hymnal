Hymn 30

SƐ AHOTEFOƆ SEN KƆ MU A
WHEN THE SAINTS GO MARCHING IN

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Meyɛ ɔkwantuni brɛfoɔ
Nenam bɔne wiase yi mu
Resiesie me ho ama kuro no
Sɛ ahotefoɔ sen kɔ mu a

Sɛ mehu Agyenkwa no a
Sɛ sɛ me bu bra foforɔ
Ansa na me ka anigye kuo n'ho
Sɛ agotefoɔ sen kɔ mu a.

Mɛdi me Gyefo no akyi
Mma, ɔm a wɔagya no mu
Saa na mehu sɛ ɔbɛfrɛ me
Sɛ ahotefoɔ sen kɔ mu a.

Ɔsoro hɔ mehu m'Agyenkwa a
ɔgye me kra fi bɔne m'
Na ɔbɛyɛ me atuu
Sɛ sen ahotefoɔ kɔ mu a.


CHORUS:
Sɛ ahotefoɔ sen kɔ mu a
Sɛ ahotefoɔ sen kɔ mu a
Wora mepɛ sɛ me ka ho bi
Sɛ ahotefoɔ sen kɔ mu a.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

I am just a weary pilgrim,
Plodding thru this world of sin
Getting ready for that city
When the saints go marching in.

If I will see the Savior,
A new life I must begin
Then I'll be in that glad number
When the saints go marching in.

I'll follow my Redeemer,
In the steps where He has been
Thus, I know that He will call me
When the saints go marching in.

Up there I will see the Savior
Who redeemed my soul from sin
With extended hands, He'll greet me
When the saints go marching in.
