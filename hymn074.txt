Hymn 74

MONYI NYAME HENE AYƐ
LET US WITH A GLADSOME MIND

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Monyi Nyame hene aye
Monfa aye yie nnwom mma no

Monhyira no; wayɛ owia
Sɛ ɛmmɔ aporɔ daa nyinaa.

Na wayɛ; ɛsrane nso
ɛhyerɛn anadwofa.

Monhyira no sɛ ɔtɛ nsuo
Na ɔma aduan fifi.

Na ɔma asase pa
Yi ne nnɔbae nso ma yɛn.

Wama twaberɛ aba
Wahyɛ yɛn asɛn mama.

Wama aduan a ɛsen sɛe
Daa anigyeɛ ho bɛhyɛ.

Anuonyam nka hene, dɛɛfoɔ
Abɔdeɛ nto anuonyam
Anuonyam nka Agya, ɔba
Ne Honhom, Baasakro no.


CHORUS:
N'ahummɔbɔ tim hɔ daa
Esi pi, yɛ nokware daa.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Let us with a gladsome mind
Praise the Lord, for he is kind

Let us blaze his name abroad
For of gods He is the God

He with all-commanding might
Filled the new-made world with light

All things living he doth feed
His full hand supplies their need

Let us then with gladsome mind
Praise the Lord, for he is kind
