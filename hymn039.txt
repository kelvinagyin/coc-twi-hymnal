Hymn 39

YƐNE NYAME NANTE
TRUST AND OBEY

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Yɛne Nyame nante
Wɔ n'asɛm no hann mu a
Anuonyam bia a ohwie gu yɛn kwan mu
Sɛ yɛyɛ ne pɛ de a
ɔne yɛn te hɔ daa
Ne obiara a ɔgyedi na ɔtie

Esum bi rensɛre, Munumkum bi rensi,
Mmom N'animteɛ papa no ntɛm so
Akyinnyɛɛ ne ohu, Apini ne nusuo
Remma sɛ yɛ gyedi na yɛtie a.

Yɛ'adesoa renyɛ kwa, Yɛn wrɛ renho kwa
ɔbɛma yɛn so akatua pa, Awerɛhɔɔ ne adehwere m'
Abufuo ne aman m', ɔbɛhyira yɛn sɛ yɛ gyedi na yɛtie a.

Na yɛrentumi nhunu
Ne dɔ mu anigye
Gyesɛ yɛde bribi abɔ afɔdeɛ
Adɔeɛ a ɔyɛ, anigye a ɔde ma
Wɔ hɔ ma wɔn a wɔgyedi na wɔtie.

Yɔnkofa dɛɛde mu
Yɛbɛtena ne naase
Anaa yɛ ne no bɛnam kwan mu
Yɛbɛyɛ deɛ ɔsɛ
Na yɛakɔ ne soma
Ensuro, gyedi na tie no nko.


CHORUS:
Gyedi na tie
ɔkwan biara nni hɔ a
Yenya Yesu mu ahotɔ
Gyesɛ w'gyedi na w'tie.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

When we walk with the Lord, in the light of His word,
What a glory He sheds on our way,
while we do His good will, He abides with us still,
and with all who will trust and obey.

Not a shadow can rise, not a cloud in the skies,
But His smile quickly drives it away;
Not a doubt or a fear, not a sigh or a tear,
Can abide while we trust and obey.

Not a burden we bear, not a sorrow we share,
But our toil He doth richly repay;
Not a grief or a loss, not a frown or a cross,
But is blest if we trust and obey.

But we never can prove the delights of His love,
Until all on the altar we lay; for the favor He shows
And the joy He bestows,
Are for those who will trust and obey.

Then in fellowship sweet we will sit at His feet,
Or we'll walk by His side in the way;
What He says, we will do, where He sends we will go
Never fear, only trust and obey.
