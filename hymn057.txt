Hymn 57

AWURADE NE ME DWANHWƐFOƆ
THE LORD'S MY SHEPHERD

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Awurade ne me dwanhwɛfoɔ
Merenhia biribi, ɔma me da wura frɔmm mu
ɔgya me nsutae ho.

ɔde me kra san ba nkwam bio
Na ɔma me nante, ne nneyɛɛ trenee kwan no so
N'ankasa ne din nti

Menam owuo sum bɔn mu mpo a
Mensuro mmusuo biara, wone me nante, wo poma
Ne w'abaa hyɛ me den.

Me pono nso woawie to deda
Wɔ matamfo anim, wode ngo fɔ me tiri mu
Me kuruwa bu so.

Yie ne adɔe bedi makyi
Me kwa nna nyinaa mu
Na mede Nyame fie bɛyɛ
Me tenabea daa nyinaa.



----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

The Lord's my Shepherd, I'll not want;
He makes me down to lie
In pastures green;
He leadeth me the quiet waters by.

My soul He doth restore again,
and me to walk doth make
Within the paths of righteousness,
E'en for His own name's sake.

Yea, tho' I walk in death's dark vale,
Yet will I fear none ill,
For Thou art with me, and Thy rod
And staff me comfort still.

My table Thou hast furnished
In presence of my foes,
My head Thou dost with oil anoint
And my cup overflows.

Goodness and mercy all my life
Shall surely follow me,
And in God's house forever more
My dwelling place shall be.
