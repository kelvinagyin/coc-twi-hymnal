Hymn 45

TETE BO A WƆPAE MAA ME
ROCK OF AGES

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Tete Bo a wɔpae maa me
Ma menguan nhunta wo mu
Ma nsuo ne mogya no a
ɛfi wo mfem resen no
ɛny bɔne ho aduro
ɛmpa me nfom nsan me ho.

Sɛ me nisuo sen daa po
Ne me mmɔden ngo da po
ɛntumi bɔne ka tua
Gyefoɔ ne Wo ara nko
Me nsam nni akatua bi
Mekuta W'asennua mu.

Megu ahome tia yi
Meka m'ani gu so a
Mɛsɛre ba no ho a
Behu Wo w'ahennwa so a
Tete Bo a wɔpae maa me
Ma menguan nhunta wo mu.



----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Rock of Ages, cleft for me,
Let me hide myself in Thee,
Let the water and the blood
From Thy wounded side which flowed,

Could my tears forever flow,
Could my zeal no languor know,
These for sin could not atone,
Thou must save, and Thou alone;

While I draw this fleeting breath,
When my eyelids close in death,
When I rise to worlds unknown,
And behold God on the throne.
