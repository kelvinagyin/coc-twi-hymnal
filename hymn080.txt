Hymn 80

GYE WƆN A WƆAYERA WƆN A WƆREWU
RESCUE THE PERISHING

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Gye wɔn a wɔayera wɔn a wɔrewu
Hwim wɔn firi bɔne ne damoa mu
Su ma deɛ wafom kwan, ma asehwefoɔ so
Ka Yesu, Gyefoɔ ho asɛm kyerɛ wɔn.

Wɔdin ne ho fɛw deɛ ɛda so retwɛn
ɛ'twɛn ɛba hohwini a wabɔ ko
Se wɔn aniberɛ so, se wɔn agobreasem'
Sɛ wɔgyedi na wɔtie a ɛde bɛkyɛ.

Onipa koma mu papa bi a wɔ hɔ
Nyame dom tumi ma efi yiye
ɛte sɛ sanku a ne dwom atwa sin
ɛdɔ nsa ka noa ɛwoso bio.

Gye wɔn a wɔayera, ɛyɛ wadwuma
Awurade bɛma wo ho ahoɔden
Fa boasetɔ twe wɔn si kwan teaa no so
Sɛ adobɔfoɔ sɛ Gyefoɔ awu.


CHORUS:
Gye wɔn a wɔayera, wɔn a wɔreyera.
Yesu yɛ Agyenkwa
Mmɔbɔhunfoɔ


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Rescue the perishing
Care for the dying
Snatch them in pity from sin and the grave;
Weep o’er the erring one
Lift up the fallen
Tell them of Jesus the mighty to save.

Though they are slighting Him
Still He is waiting
Waiting the penitent child to receive;
Plead with them earnestly
Plead with them gently;
He will forgive if they only believe.

Down in the human heart
Crushed by the tempter
Feelings lie buried that grace can restore;
Touched by a loving heart
Wakened by kindness
Chords that are broken will vibrate once more.

Rescue the perishing
Duty demands it;
Strength for thy labor the Lord will provide;
Back to the narrow way
Patiently win them;
Tell the poor wand’rer a Savior has died.
