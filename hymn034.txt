IS IT FOR ME, DEAR SAVIOR?

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

W'anuonyam ne wo home
ɛyɛ me de Agyenkwa pa
Me a mayɛ mmere bɔne mu
So wo bɛhyira me sɛ?

so me na wo frɛ me yi
Sɛ me mmra w'adom mu yi?
Me a bone ahyɛ me ma yi
Na wo frɛ me hyirafo?

O Agyenkwa a wo som bo
Mekoma da won an ase
Mɛhyira na madɔ wo
Na mɛpre m'ahyia wo.

Mɛtena wo nkyɛn mmere nyinaa
Na merenho wo werɛ bio
Mɛkamfo wo Agyenkwa pa
Na madɔ wo mmere nyinaa.


CHORUS:
M'Agyenkwa pa, ne me Gyefoɔ
Deɛn na mɛtumi aye?
Sɛ mɛkamfo atontom wo

ENGLISH VERSION

Is it for me, dear Savior,
Thy glory and thy rest
For me, so weak and sinful?
O shall I be so blest?

Is it for me, thy welcome,
Thy gracious "Enter in"
For me, thy "Come, ye blessed,"
For me so full of sin?

O Savior, precious Savior,
My heart is at thy feet
I bless thee, and I love thee,
And thee I long to meet.

I'll be with thee forever,
And never grieve thee more;
Dear Savior, I must praise thee,
And love thee evermore.
