Hymn 20

MONKAMFO YESU
PRAISE HIM, PRAISE HIM

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Monkamfo no, Yesu yen Gyefo Hyirafoɔ
Asase mo nto nnwom nyi ne dɔ adi
Soro abɔfo mo mpene no animuonyam mu
Monfa nnidie ma ne din kronkron no
Se ɔhwɛfoɔ pa Yesu bɛhwɛ ne nnwan so
ɔbɛkora wɔn ne nsam daa nyinaa
Monkamfo no, mo nyi ne kɛseyɛ adi
Monfa ahurisie nnwom nkamfo no daa.

monkamfo no, Yesu yen Gyefo Hyirafo
Yɛn bɔne nti ɔhuu amane na owui
Yɛn botantim, yen daa nkwagye anidasoɔ
Mo mpene Yesu a wɔbɔ ɔɔN'asennua mu
Mo nyi no aye, Yesu soaa yen bɔne nyinaa
Anwanwadɔ emu dɔ na ɛyɛ den
Monkamfo no, monyi ne kɛseyɛ adi
Monfa ahurisi nnwom nkamfo no daa.

monkamfo no, Yesu yen Gyefo Hyirafoɔ
Hosanna denden gyegye ɔsoro
Agyenkwa Yesu te ase daadaa ne daa
Monkamfo yen Sɔfo ne Hene no
Kristo reba, wadi wiase so nkunim
Tumi ne animuonyam ye Awurade dea
Monkamfo no, monyi ne kɛseyɛ adi
Monfa ahurisi nnwom nkamfo no daa.



----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Praise Him; praise Him, our blessed Redeemer
Sing, O earth His wonderful love proclaim
Hail Him; hail Him, highest arch angels in glory,
Strength and honor give to His holy name
Like a shepherd, His greatness, praise Him,
Praise Him ever in joyful song
Jesus will guide His children,
In His arms He carries them all day long.

Praise Him; praise Him, our blessed Redeemer
For our sins He suffered, and bled and died;
He our rock, our hope of eternal salvation,
Hail Him, hail Him, Jesus the crucified,
Sound His praises, Jesus who bore our sorrows,
Love unbounded, wonderful, deep and strong;

Praise Him; praise Him, Jesus, our blessed Redeemer
Heavenly portals, loud with hosannas ring
Jesus, Savior, liveth forever and ever;
Praise Him, praise Him, prophet and priest and King;
Christ is coming over the world victorious,
Pow'r and glory unto the Lord belong
