O FOR A THOUSAND TONGUES TO SING

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Anka me nya tɛkyɛrɛma apem
M'ayi me Gyefo aye
M'akamfo me Hene anuonyam
N'adom ne nkonimdie.

Me wura dɛɛfoɔ me Nyankopɔn
Boa me memɔ dawuro
Memfa wo din ho nidie so
Menhyehyɛ mmaa nyinaa.

Yesu din a ɛtu yɛn suro
ɛyi yɛn yea nyinaa
Gyegye dɛdɛ bɔnefo asom
ɛyɛ nkwa ne asomdwoe

ɔkasa na wote ne nne a
Awufoɔ nya nkwa bio
Awerɛhofoɔ nya anigye
Hiani brɛfoɔ gye di.

ɔdwiri bɔne tumi koraa
ɔtu daduani duam
Ne mogya te bɔnefoɔ ho
ɛdi nim ma me nso.

Me bɔne nyinaa da Yesu so
Wɔkum Nyame Adwamma
Wɔde ne kra bɔɔ afɔdeɛ
Maa nipa biara kra.

ENGLISH VERSION

O for a thousand tongues to sing,
Blessed be the name of the Lord,
The glories of my God and King
Blessed be the name of the Lord.

Jesus, the name that charms our fears,
Blessed be the name of the Lord,
'Tis music in the sinner's ears,
Blessed be the name of the Lord.

He died to save my sinsick soul
Blessed be the name of the Lord
His blood can make the foulest whole
Blessed be the name of the Lord.

I never shall forget that day,
Blessed be the name of the Lord
When Jesus washed my sins away,
Blessed be the name of the Lord.
