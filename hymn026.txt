Hymn 26

YƐ NE NYINAA AWURADE DIN MU
DO ALL IN THE NAME OF THE LORD

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Wo kasa ne wo nneyɛɛ mu
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu
ɛnyɛ biribi nnipa nhyehyɛe mu
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu.

ɛmma wiase ɛnaadaa wo
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu
Honhom no se 'kasam' neyɛe m'
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu.

Sɛ wo rebrɛ apɛ ahenkyɛ a
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu
O mf wo ho nto wiase so
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu.

Kɔsi sɛ ase ha adwuma bɛsa
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu
Adristofo, mo pɛ koroye a
Monya ne nyinaa Awurade din mu.


CHORUS:
Yɛ ne nyinaa ne din mu
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu
Sɛ deɛ Nyama ahyɛhyɛ no
Yɛ ne nyinaa Awurade din mu.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Whate'er you do in word or deed,
Do all in the name of the Lord;
Do naught in name of man or creed
Do all in the name of the Lord.

Be not deceived by worldly greed,
Do all in the name of the Lord,
The Spirit says in word or deed,
Do all in the name of the Lord.

If you are toiling for a crown,
Do all in the name of the Lord
O do not trust in world renown,
Do all in the name of the Lord.

Till toil and labors here are done,
Do all in the name of the Lord
Dear Christian friends, if you'd be one,
Do all in the name of the Lord.
