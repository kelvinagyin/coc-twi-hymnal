YESU KRISTO RESEN HA

TWI VERSION

Yesu Kristo resen ha
Bɔneyɛni to w'ani hwɛ
Berɛ pa no retwam yi
Kekam se, hu me mmɔbɔ.

Yesu Kristo resen ha
So ɔbɛbɛn wo sɛɛ daa?
ɛnnɛ ne ɔgye da no
Hwehwɛ nkwa na wobenya.

ɔgyina hɔ refrɛ wo
ɔse: Menyɛ dɛn mma wo?
Srɛ da wo hia sɛm kyɛn'
ɔrefrɛ wo ampa ara.

Mepɛ sɛ mehub Wadom
Me wura yi wo do kyere me
Ma ɛnhwire me kra mu
ɛnhyɛ koma ne kra so.
