Hymn 75

KOKO BI SI NOHO ARA


----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Koko bi si nohoa ara
Kuro kɛseɛ n'akyi hɔ
Nea wɔsɛn yɛn wura no
Owui gyee yɛn nkwa no.

Yɛnim nso yɛrentumi nka
Yea kɛseɛ a ɔhui
Nanso yɛgyedi sɛ yɛn nti
Na wɔsɛn no wɔ ho.

Obiara nni hɔ a ɔyɛ a
Obɛtumi bɔne ayi
ɔno nko na ɔbɛtumi abue
ɔsoro pon ama yɛn.

O W'adɔuɛn dɔ kɛse pa
ɛsɛ sɛ yɛdɔ no
Na yɛatwere ne mogya no
Na yɛaye n'adwuma.
