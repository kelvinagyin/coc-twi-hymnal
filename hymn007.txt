Hymn 7

MÉTIE
I'LL BE LISTENING

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Se Agyenkwa no fré a megye so
Se ofré me a mète ne nne
Se Agyenkwa no fé a megye so
Megyina baabi na m'atie me din.

Se makoma tene na ofré me
Se makoma tene a mète ne nne
Se makoma tene na ofré me
Megyina baabi na matie me din.

Se matade hyerén na ofré me
Se matade hyerén a me ten e nne
Se matade hyeré na ofré me
Megyina baabi na matie me din.


CHORUS:
Megyina baabi matie
Megyina baabi matie
Megyina baabi na matie me din.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

When the Savior calls I will answer,
When He calls for me I will hear;
When the Savior calls I will answer,
I'll be somewhere list'ning for my name

if my heart is right when He calls me,
If my heart is right I will hear;
If my heart is right when He calls me,
I'll be somewhere list'ning for my name.

if my robe is white when He calls me,
If my robe is white I will hear;
If my robe is white when He calls me,
I'll be somewhere list'ning for my name.
