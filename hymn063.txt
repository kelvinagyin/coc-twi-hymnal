Hymn 63

NYAME NE WO NTENA KOSI SƐ
GOD BE WITH YOU

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Nyame ne wo ntena kosi sɛ yɛbɛhyia bio
ɔmfa n'adwen nkyerɛw kwan, nkora w'so
ɔmfa w'nka ne nwan ho nwɛn wo
Nyame ne wo ntena kosi sɛ yɛbɛhyia bio.

Nyame ne wo ntena kosi sɛ yɛbɛhyia bio
ɔmfa n'ataban nkata w'so
ɔma w'manna mfa nyɛn wo kra
Nyame ne wo ntena kosi sɛ yɛbɛhyia bio.

Nyame ne wo ntena kosi sɔ yɛbɛhyia bio
ɔmfa 'dɔ frankaa nsi wo so
ɔmɔ owuo ho suro mfi wanim
Nyame ne wo ntena kosi sɛ yɛbɛhyia bio.


CHORUS:
Yɛbɛhyia, yɛbɛhyia
Yɛbɛhyia, wɔ Yesu nan ase
Yɛbɛhyia, yɛbɛhyia bio
Nyame ne wo ntena kosi sɛ yɛbɛhyia bio


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

God be with you till we meet again,
By His counsels guide uphold you
With His sheep securely fold you
God be with you till we meet again.

God be with you till we meet again,
Neath His wings securely hide you
Daily manna still provide you
God be with you till we meet again.

God be with you till we meet again,
Keep love's banner floating o'er you
Smite death's threatening wave before you
God be with you till we meet again.
