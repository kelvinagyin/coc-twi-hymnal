Hymn 16

NISUO NNI SORO
NO TEARS IN HEAVEN

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Nisuo nni soro, awereho nni hɔ
ɛhɔ beye anuonyam man
Awerho mma hɔ anigye nkutoo
Se yɛkɔ dɔm anigye kuo no.

Anuonyam retwɛn, ɛretwɛn nohoaa
ɛhɔ na ade rensa da
Ye ne yen Agyenkwa betena daa daa
Na nisuo mmu aba mu bio.

Da bi anɔpa yɛbɛgyae adwendwen
deɛ yɛahu ɔbra yi mu ho
yɛbɛhu bribia, 'yɛadɔ ahotefoɔ
ade nyinaa beyɛ foforo.


CHORUS:
Nisuo nni soro aheman
Nisuo nni soro hɔ
awereho ne yea nyinaa bɛsa
nisuo nni sor'ahemman
nisuo nni soro hɔ
obi nte nisuo wɔ soro.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

No tears in heaven, no sorrows given,
All will be glory in that land;
There'll be no sadness, all will be gladness,
When we shall join that happy band.

Glory is waiting, waiting up yonder,
Where we shall spend an endless day;
There with our Savior we'll be forever,
Where no more sorrow can dismay.

Some morning yonder, we'll cease to ponder
O'er things this life has bro't to view;
All will be clearer, saved ones be dearer,
In heav'n where all will be made new.
