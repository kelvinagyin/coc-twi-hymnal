Hymn 17

ƆDƆ ANWANWASƐM
WONDERFUL STORY OF LOVE

----------------------------

TWI VERSION

Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa,
Siso kyere me bio
Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa
ɛkanyaa me kra bio
abɔfo de anigye ka
nwanhwɛfɔg ye no suro so
bɔneyɛfo wo nnye nni anaa?
Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa.

Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa
se wo wɔ akyiri mpo a
Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa
Ɔda so frɛ wo ɛnnɛ
Ɔfrɛ fi Kalvary bepɔ so
Fi abura hyɛnhyɛn no mu
ɛfi abɔde ahyease
Ɔdɔ asɛm nwanwa.

Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa
Yesu de ɔhome
Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa
Ma deɛ wahyira wɔn
yɛn ne wɔn a wɔadi kan no
bɛhome ɔsoro fie mu
yɛbɛto anigye dwom ho.
Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa.


CHORUS:
nwanwasɛm, nwanwasɛm
nwanwasɛm ni
Ɔdɔ ho asɛm nwanwa.


----------------------------

ENGLISH VERSION

Wonderful story of love,
Tell it to me again,
Wonderful story of love,
Wake the immortal strain;
Angels with rapture announce it,
Shepherds with wonder receive it;
Sinner, O won't you believe it?
Wonderful story of love.

Wonderful story of love,
Tho' you are far away,
Wonderful story of love,
Still He doth call today,
Calling from Calvary's mountain
Down from the crystal bright fountain,
E'en from the dawn of creation,
Wonderful story of love.

Wonderful story of love,
Jesus provides a rest,
Wonderful story of love,
For all the pure and blest;
Rest in those mansions above us
With those who've gone on before us,
Singing the rapturous chorus,
Wonderful story of love.
